Every panel, from release to install.

CladLogic Released tracks each facade release from the CAD handoff through the shop, onto the truck and up the wall, so the PM, CAD, the shop and the field crew are working from the same picture.

Sign in Get the iPhone and iPad app

What it keeps track of

Releases drive everything. Each one carries its panels, documents and approvals from the office to the job site.

Releases

CAD releases panels with their drawings, scopes and approvals. Revisions replace what came before, so nobody builds from an old sheet.

The shop

The shop scans a release's QR code to open it, logs cut and fab progress, and crates what's ready to go.

The field

Drivers move crates to site, and foremen and installers see what has landed and what's left to hang.

Who uses it

Project managers, CAD, the shop, drivers, foremen, installers, superintendents and general managers each see the part of a release they're responsible for, on the web or on iPhone and iPad.
